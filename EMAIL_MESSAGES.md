# User Email Messages

This document contains the email content the user should receive based on the activity happening in the system.

---

## 1) Register

Subject: Welcome to LFC New Jerusalem, Ilorin – Your account has been created

Dear [First Name],

Welcome to LFC New Jerusalem, Ilorin – The State Church. We are so glad to have you with us.

Your account has been successfully created, and you are now one step closer to joining the church community and accessing our services.

To complete your registration and activate your account, please verify your email address using the one-time PIN sent to you.

If you did not create this account, please ignore this message or contact our support team immediately.

We look forward to walking with you in faith and fellowship.

Blessings,
LFC New Jerusalem, Ilorin
The State Church

---

## 2) Verify Endpoint OTP

Subject: Your email verification code

Dear [First Name],

## 3) Login

Subject: Login notification

This is to inform you that your account was accessed successfully on [Date] at [Time].

LFC New Jerusalem, Ilorin
The State Church

[Reset Password Link]

## Optional: Short SMS/Push Variant

These can also be used for SMS or in-app notifications:

- Register: "Your LFC account has been created. Please verify your email to complete setup."
- OTP: "Your verification code is 123456. Valid for 10 minutes."
- Login: "A successful login was detected on your account. If this was not you, reset your password immediately."
- Forgot password: "Reset your password here: [link]"
- Approved: "Your application has been approved. Please check your email for the next steps."
- Rejected: "Your application status update is available. Please check your email."
- Pending: "Your application is still under review. We will notify you once a decision is made."
